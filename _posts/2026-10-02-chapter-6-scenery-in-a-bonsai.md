Chapter 6, **Scenery in a Bonsai** (盆中景), is later life.

## The idea

You've left the house. Out here there's only grassland, wide and quiet, and for the first time there's no hand. Nothing is chasing you.

Walk a little further and you start to find graves: no names, just small carvings. Then more of them. The sun goes down, the grass turns gold, a meteor falls. Somewhere on the horizon, someone with a lantern is waiting.

## Reading the image

- **The palette** is faded straw-gold and ash, a sunset over a sea of grass. The only other colours are a ribbon of green aurora, the white streak of a meteor, and the thin red line of wildfire far away.
- **The graves** in the foreground are carved with butterflies, silkworms and cocoons. Two are shaped like open hands. Beside some lie masks that were finally taken off.
- **The traveller**, old now and leaning on a stick, walks among them in the same white cloak.
- **The miniatures** are the whole journey so far, small enough to hold: the rope bridge, the boat with its glowing door, the cocoon on the pine, the teapot mountain, the red-roofed house. If you look closely, there's someone by the lake watching the fish.
- **The bonsai pot** is the frame: its glazed rim curves along the bottom of the image, and the great pine leans over everything.

On the far right, at the edge of the fire, a single figure holds up a lantern.
