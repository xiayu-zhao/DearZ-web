Development officially starts today.

- Set up a new **Unity 6.3** project and indexed it with **Cursor** so the AI assistant can navigate the codebase.
- Imported a handful of free environment and character assets as stand-ins. None of this is final art — it's just enough to have something to move around.
- With Cursor's help, wired up basic **WASD movement** — walk, run and jump — with animation.

It's a grey box and a borrowed mannequin, but it moves. That's the foundation everything else will stand on.
