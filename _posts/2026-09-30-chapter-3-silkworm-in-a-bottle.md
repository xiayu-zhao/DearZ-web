Chapter 3, **Silkworm in a Bottle** (瓶中蚕), is adolescence.

## The idea

The desert from the end of Chapter 2 goes on. Far ahead, a city of white domes floats in the sky. The people who live there are born with robes that let them fly, and they come and go as easily as breathing. You can't fly. Neither can the traveller who offers you a ride on their camel, and who turns out to be a lot more like you than either of you expected.

Getting up there means finding out what you are, and then becoming it.

## Reading the image

- **The palette** is bleached sand and ivory, a single warm ink tone. The only colour is the muted green of mulberry leaves, and this chapter's light is green.
- **The silk tunnel** on the left is the inside of a cocoon: thousands of threads spun into a passage, with more cocoons hanging like lanterns. A tiny figure stands deep inside it.
- **The riders** in white robes cross the dunes toward the city, leaving a long line of footprints behind them.
- **The curved wall of glass** is both the barrier at the edge of the desert and a hint that this whole world sits inside a jar, lit from its open mouth above.

After the night-blue sea of Chapter 2, this is the brightest place the journey has been so far. That's deliberate: adolescence is full of light, even when it's hard.
