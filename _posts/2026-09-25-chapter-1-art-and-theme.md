Chapter 1, **Bridge to the Afterlife** (往生桥), finally has a face — and a voice.

## The art

The chapter is built from a few images I keep coming back to: a mountain gorge thick with mist, a lantern-bearer who never quite shows their face, a field of red spider lilies — the flowers said to grow along the river between worlds — and a rope bridge across the water.

The palette is almost entirely grey-green ink. Color is rare in *Dear Z.*, and when it appears it means something: here, the red of the lilies, and the nine threads of light at the far end of the bridge.
