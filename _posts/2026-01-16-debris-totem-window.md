Today the debris started to *do* something.

**The loop:**

1. Collect enough **Debris** and it forms a **Totem**.
2. **Drag** the totem onto an **Anchor** placed in the level.
3. The anchor opens a **Window** — in this prototype, one of two variants, *Window A* and *Window B*.

I built prefab models for `Win_A` and `Win_B` so windows can be dropped into any scene, and added sound effects for every step of the process — pickup, totem forming, drag, and the window opening.

In the full game, a Window is a tear in time: a way to reach a hidden place, a lost object, or a person from somewhere else in Z's memory. This is the very first time it has existed outside a design doc.
