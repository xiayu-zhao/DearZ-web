Three additions today:

- **Attack** — a basic melee swing.
- **Double jump** — because platforming needs a little air.
- **Debris** — the game's core collectible. Floating points of light are scattered along the path; walking through them picks them up, and the UI (top-left circles, and a counter in the corner) fills as you go.

Debris is the heart of *Dear Z.* In the finished game each fragment is a piece of Z's memory, tinted by the emotion it carries. For now they're simple glowing spheres — but they already feel good to collect, which is the first thing that has to be right.
