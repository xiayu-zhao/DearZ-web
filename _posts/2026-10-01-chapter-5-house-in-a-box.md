Chapter 5, **House in a Box** (盒中屋), is midlife.

## The idea

Halfway up the mountain there's a grand old house. By day, its servants work the fields as oxen and horses, with ploughs strapped to their backs and chains on their ankles. At night, they come home and put on their masks.

Inside, everyone looks the same and walks the same way, in one long line. Before the sun goes down, anyone still walking alone is put outside, where the hand is waiting. So people keep offering you a chain: just tie yourself to someone, and you can stay.

## Reading the image

- **The palette** is aged cinnabar lacquer: oxblood, rust and dark umber, with the last of the sunset bleeding across the horizon. Home is warm here, and it's also suffocating.
- **The courtyard** is built around a grave. A grassy mound and a stone stele sit at its centre, and the household moves around it as if it were nothing unusual.
- **The procession** circles the courtyard in masks, many walking in pairs joined at the ankle.
- **The traveller** is the only figure in white and the only one unmasked. They're holding a mask that someone has just handed them, and haven't put it on yet.
- **The stage** on the left shows a shadow-play, two figures from an old story leaning toward each other.
- **The box** is the frame itself: carved red lacquer walls at the edges, and a lid propped open overhead, letting in the final light.

On the right, beyond the gate, the hand of mist rises out of the storm. It's waiting for sundown.
